A migration that moves everything at once is a migration that goes wrong at once. We plan migrations as a series of small, reversible moves, starting with the workloads that teach you the most for the least risk.

Along the way we build the foundations properly: account structure, networking, identity and infrastructure as code. When the last workload moves, you're left with an environment that's easy to grow, not one you need to tidy up later.

## Already in the cloud but not getting the benefit?

Lift-and-shift often lands teams with the cloud bill and none of the agility. We help you modernise in place, containerising, automating and adopting managed services where they make sense.
